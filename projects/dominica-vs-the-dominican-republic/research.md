# Research: Dominica vs the Dominican Republic

## Core Claim
Dominica and the Dominican Republic are two completely different countries. Dominica is a small Eastern Caribbean island (750 km², ~72,000 people), while the Dominican Republic is a large Hispanic nation on Hispaniola (48,671 km², ~10.8 million people). The only thing they share is the word "Dominica" in their names.

| Fact | Source |
|------|--------|
