# Script: Dominica vs the Dominican Republic

## Template v2 — Kinetic Short (~35s, 9:16)

---

### Scene 1 — Hook (1.5s)
**Visual:** glmap highlight Dominica + flag, label "DOMINICA?", strike through, highlight Dominican Republic + flag, label "DOMINICAN REPUBLIC?"
**Audio:** "Dominica and the Dominican Republic. Same name, completely different countries."

---

### Scene 2 — Answer (1.5s)
**Visual:** highlight Dominica (small), label "750 km², 72K people, English"; highlight Dominican Republic (large), label "48,671 km², 10.8M people, Spanish"
**Audio:** "One's a tiny English-speaking island. The other's a massive Spanish-speaking nation. They're not even on the same island."

---

### Scene 3 — Open Loop (1s)
**Visual:** highlight both, label "SAME NAME?", pulse
**Audio:** "How did two countries end up with almost the same name?"

---

### Scene 4 — Story: Columbus & Santo Domingo (3s)
**Visual:** highlight Dominica, label "COLUMBUS 1493 - SUNDAY", stamp "DOMINICA"; highlight Hispaniola, label "SANTO DOMINGO 1496", stamp "DOMINICAN REPUBLIC"
**Audio:** "Columbus spots an island on a Sunday, November 3, 1493. Names it Dominica. Three years later, his brother founds Santo Domingo on Hispaniola. That becomes the Dominican Republic."

---

### Scene 5 — Aside (1.5s)
**Visual:** label "150x THE POPULATION", strike through, label "65x THE SIZE"
**Audio:** "One has 150 times the population. 65 times the size. Guess which is which."

---

### Scene 6 — Divergence (3s)
**Visual:** highlight Dominica, label "FRENCH → BRITISH", stamp "INDEPENDENCE 1978"; highlight Dominican Republic, label "HAITI OCCUPATION", stamp "INDEPENDENCE 1844"
**Audio:** "Dominica flips between French and British, independent 1978. Dominican Republic: Haitian occupation, then independence 1844. Two different histories, two different worlds."

---

### Scene 7 — Twist (2s)
**Visual:** highlight both capitals, label "ROSEAU" on Dominica, label "SANTO DOMINGO" on Dominican Republic, pulse, label "NEITHER IS DOMINICA"
**Audio:** "The twist — Dominica's capital is Roseau. Dominican Republic's capital is Santo Domingo. Neither is actually called Dominica."

---

### Scene 8 — Punchline (2s)
**Visual:** both flags side by side, subscribe button (top), label "NOT THE SAME"
**Audio:** "Same root word. Two different worlds. And no, they don't play each other in soccer."

---

### Scene 9 — Loop (1s)
**Visual:** both flags fade to opening frame, silent subscribe button
**Audio:** "And to this day..."
