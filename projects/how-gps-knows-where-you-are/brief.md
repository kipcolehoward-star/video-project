# How GPS Knows Where You Are

Created: 2026-10-06

## Goal
The viewer leaves understanding that GPS is fundamentally a **timing** system:
position comes from measuring how long signals take to arrive — and that this
only works because of Einstein's relativity corrections.

## Audience
Curious general audience. No maths beyond "distance = time × speed".

## Deliverables
- Primary format: 16:9, ~75–90 s
- Captions: documentary (burned in) + SRT/VTT for upload
- No cutdowns for now

## Tone and style
Calm documentary explainer. Typography, numbers and one diagram carry the
visuals — no stock footage (none licensed yet). One idea per scene.

## Must include / must avoid
- Include: atomic clocks, delay → distance, trilateration + the 4th satellite,
  the 1 µs ≈ 300 m sensitivity, relativity (−7 / +45 / +38 µs per day), ~10 km/day.
- Avoid: overstating accuracy (phones ≈ 4.9 m in open sky, worse near buildings),
  exact satellite counts that go stale (say "more than thirty").
- Music: none until a licensed track is supplied (audio.music in project.json).
