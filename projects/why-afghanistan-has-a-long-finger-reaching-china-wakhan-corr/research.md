# Research: Why Afghanistan Has a Long Finger Reaching China (Wakhan Corridor)

## Core Claim
Afghanistan's Wakhan Corridor is a narrow 350 km panhandle created as a buffer zone between the Russian and British Empires during the Great Game (1873-1893). It gives Afghanistan a border with China but remains one of the world's most remote inhabited places.

## Key Facts & Sources

| Fact | Source |
|------|--------|
| **Wakhan Corridor dimensions** — ~350 km long, 13-65 km wide, total area ~10,000 km² | [Encyclopaedia Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/Wakhan-Corridor), [NASA Earth Observatory](https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/images/88394/wakhan-corridor) |
| **Created as buffer zone** — 1873 Anglo-Russian agreement, 1893 Durand Line finalized it; purpose: prevent Russian and British Empires from sharing a border | [Oxford Encyclopedia of Empire](https://www.oxfordreference.com/), [The Great Game by Peter Hopkirk] |
| **Borders China** — Only 76 km border with China (Xinjiang) at the eastern tip (Wakhjir Pass, 4,923 m) | [CIA World Factbook](https://www.cia.gov/the-world-factbook/), [China-Afghanistan Border Treaty 1963] |
| **Population** — ~12,000 Wakhi and Kyrgyz people, mostly nomadic pastoralists | [World Bank Afghanistan Data], [AKDN Wakhan Project Reports] |
| **No road access** — No paved roads connect to rest of Afghanistan; only foot/animal trails or helicopter | [NATO/ISAF Reports], [National Geographic Wakhan Expedition] |
| **Marco Polo route** — Corridor was part of Silk Road; Marco Polo traveled through in 1271 | [The Travels of Marco Polo], [UNESCO Silk Road Programme] |
| **Strategic irrelevance today** — Neither China nor Tajikistan wants road through it; remains a geographic curiosity | [Carnegie Endowment Analysis], [China-Pakistan Economic Corridor routing avoids it] |
