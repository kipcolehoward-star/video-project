# Research: New Zealand Almost Became a State of Australia

## Core Claim
New Zealand was originally included in the Australian Constitution and could have become an Australian state, but chose not to join at federation in 1901.

## Key Facts & Sources

| Fact | Source |
|------|--------|
| **1891 National Australasian Convention** — NZ sent delegates (Sir John Hall, Sir George Grey) but took a "wait and see" approach | [Australian Dictionary of Biography](https://adb.anu.edu.au/biography/hall-sir-john-6392), [Te Ara Encyclopedia](https://teara.govt.nz/en/federation) |
| **1897-1898 Australasian Federal Convention** — NZ attended but did not commit; Richard Seddon (NZ PM) opposed joining | [NZ History](https://nzhistory.govt.nz/politics/federation), [Parliament of Australia](https://www.aph.gov.au/About_Parliament/Parliamentary_Departments/Parliamentary_Library/pubs/BriefingBook44p/Federation) |
| **Australian Constitution preamble (1900)** — "including New Zealand" in the list of colonies that could join | [Australian Constitution Section 6](https://www.legislation.gov.au/Details/C2013Q00005), [National Archives Australia](https://www.naa.gov.au/learn/learning-resources/learning-resource-themes/federation/constitution) |
| **Section 121 of Constitution** — "The Parliament may admit to the Commonwealth or establish new States... on such terms and conditions as it thinks fit" — explicitly includes NZ | [Australian Constitution Section 121](https://www.legislation.gov.au/Details/C2013Q00005) |
| **1900 Referendum in NZ** — Seddon government chose NOT to hold a referendum; NZ missed the 1901 federation | [NZ History](https://nzhistory.govt.nz/politics/federation), [Dictionary of NZ Biography](https://teara.govt.nz/en/biographies/2s9/seddon-richard-john) |
| **Distance** — ~2,000 km (Tasman Sea) made governance and defence integration difficult | Geographic fact: Auckland to Sydney = 2,155 km |
| **Māori Land Wars (1860s-1870s)** — NZ had different indigenous relations; Australian colonies had no treaties | [Waitangi Tribunal](https://www.waitangitribunal.govt.nz/), [Te Ara](https://teara.govt.nz/en/new-zealand-wars) |
| **Economic independence** — NZ had stronger trade with UK; Australian protectionism (tariffs) would hurt NZ wool/meat exports | [NZ Treasury historical data](https://www.treasury.govt.nz/publications/guide/new-zealand-economic-history) |
| **Current status** — Section 6 of Constitution Act still lists NZ; NZ could theoretically join tomorrow | [Australian Constitution](https://www.legislation.gov.au/Details/C2013Q00005) |
