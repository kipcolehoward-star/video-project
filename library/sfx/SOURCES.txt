SFX library — one line per file. A file without a line here is not used in videos.

Format:
folder\file-name | site | page link | licence

Example:
water\river-soft.mp3 | Pixabay | https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/... | Pixabay Content License

Folders:
water   river flow, splash, waves
whoosh  soft transitions, camera moves
pop     flags, labels appearing
impact  stamps, reveals, big moments
ui      ticks, clicks, counters
paper   cards, maps, pages
